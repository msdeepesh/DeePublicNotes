Kochi, New York, London, Paris, Seattle, San Francisco, Dublin... Seven cities from Kerala are in this list. They are in the 'Oxford Economics' list of the world's top 1,000 cities.

19 500-8 Thrissur, Kochi and Kozhikode

The index is prepared on the basis of economic condition, human resources, standard of living, environment and governance. Thrissur achieved excellent performance in economic condition, environment and governance. Meanwhile, Kochi has the best economic condition, human resources and land management, and Kottayam is ranked first among Indian cities in terms of quality of life. Kollam and Thrissur are in second and third place.
